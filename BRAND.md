# Vyavsetu Brand Notes

**Tagline:** One Ecosystem. Every Business.

## Identity direction
The selected concept uses a three-part leaf/V mark to communicate growth, technology and a connected ecosystem. This is a design direction, not a claim of trademark exclusivity.

## Colours
- Navy: #0F2D4A — trust and stability
- Green: #16C47F — growth
- Cyan: #0EA5E9 — technology
- Orange: #F59E0B — energy and progress

## Typography
Use a modern geometric sans-serif such as Poppins for brand headlines; use a system sans-serif fallback for performance and portability.

## Usage
Use the SVG mark for favicons and small digital placements. Use the full wordmark and tagline for presentations. Verify trademark and domain availability before commercial launch.
