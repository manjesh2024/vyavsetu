03 · Non-negotiable implementation rules

Enforce these as domain/API tests, not just UI conventions.

01
Server-side tenant/location isolation

Scope protected reads/writes from authenticated membership; never trust only a client-selected tenant ID.

02
Checkout revalidation

Recalculate prices, discounts, tax and stock server-side. Client totals are previews only.

03
Idempotent mutations

Stable keys for order, payment, sync and retries. Never duplicate stock movements after retry.

04
Immutable final invoices

Correct with traceable credit/refund/reversal records; keep provisional references separate from final invoice IDs.

05
Auditable inventory ledger

Record source, actor, location, quantity and reason for each movement; define offline oversell policy.

06
Sync never hides failure

Preserve local writes and expose pending/syncing/synced/failed/conflict states.

07
Print outcome is separate from sale

A printer error cannot roll back a committed sale. Reprints should be traceable; drawer rules differ for cash and online payment.
